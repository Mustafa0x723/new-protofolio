About me

I'm a Computer Science student who likes turning scattered information into something structured and easy to use.

My studies focus on data engineering fundamentals: Python, SQL, relational database design and data cleaning with Pandas. I've applied them in study projects, including a house prices data cleaning and analysis project and a student score prediction model.

I'm at the beginning of my career and haven't worked as a data engineer yet. What I can offer today is careful, well-organized work, and I use every project to practice the basics of clean data and clean structure.

My goal is to grow into a data engineer who builds reliable data pipelines. Until then, I take on freelance document and portfolio work where precise formatting matters.
